About C Morning Bakery

C Morning Bakery is a future bakery project created with a simple idea: to bring freshly baked pastries, delicious baguettes and quality breakfast treats to the morning table.

Our vision is to create a welcoming bakery where people can enjoy fresh, simple and delicious food made with care.
